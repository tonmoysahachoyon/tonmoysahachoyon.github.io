## Overview
Processed raw transactional data to derive Recency, Frequency, and Monetary (RFM) features, applying unsupervised learning to segment the customer base.

![Customer Segmentation Plot](/images/rfm-clusters.png)

## Key Highlights
* **Feature Engineering:** Extracted RFM metrics and applied logarithmic scaling to correct right-skewness in the distributions.
* **Unsupervised Learning:** Segmented the customer base into four distinct behavioral cohorts using K-Means clustering.
* **Business Impact:** Successfully isolated a high-value customer segment alongside identifying over 1,000 inactive accounts for targeted re-engagement.
